Aerial Entertainment · Skyworx Drone Shows

Turn-Key Synchronized Aerial Swarm Productions

Under the leadership of Founder & CEO Taylor Woodall, Skyworx Drone Shows delivers world-class aerial storytelling with hundreds of synchronized illuminated drones dancing across the night sky.

Precision Engineering Meets Artistic Spectacle

Directing synchronized drone entertainment requires an uncompromising commitment to spatial precision and flight safety. Every performance combines technical flight planning, 3D waypoint calculation, radio telemetry redundancy, and real-time monitoring to create breathtaking aerial visual displays for festivals, corporate launches, sports stadiums, and city celebrations.

As a licensed FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot and mechanical engineer with aerospace propulsion experience at Aerojet Rocketdyne, Taylor Woodall oversees all stages of show execution—from preliminary airspace feasibility studies to final live flight command.

Strict FAA Flight Safety

Automated geofencing, multi-layered fail-safes, dual radio links, and 100% regulatory compliance.

Sub-Centimeter RTK Positioning

Centimeter-grade RTK GPS synchronization allows dense formations and crisp 3D logos in the sky.

Audio Timecode Synchronization

Frame-accurate SMPTE timecode sync with musical scores, voiceovers, and live event lighting.

Custom 3D Choreography

Bespoke 3D animations engineered specifically for the client's brand, venue sightlines, and audience scale.

Production Workflow & Milestones

Phase 1: Creative Concept & Airspace Authorization
Weeks 1–3

Design bespoke storyboard frames, evaluate site coordinates, and obtain all necessary FAA airspace waivers and local flight permits.

Phase 2: 3D Trajectory Simulation & Deconfliction
Weeks 3–4

Run mathematical collision avoidance algorithms, verify velocity profiles, and simulate dynamic lighting transitions in software.

Phase 3: On-Site Setup, Calibration & Live Flight
Show Day

Establish ground RTK base station, deploy fleet grid, run pre-flight sensor checks, and execute flawless live show command.
