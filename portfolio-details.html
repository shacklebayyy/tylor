Project Overview & Technical Execution

Under the direction of Taylor Woodall, Skyworx, LLC implemented advanced imaging and spatial data-processing workflows to execute high-density aerial LiDAR surveys and orthomosaic photogrammetry for commercial and industrial clients.

Operational Challenges

Surveying complex multi-elevation terrain with steep canopy cover, dense vegetation, and variable barometric conditions required penetration through foliage to establish accurate bare-earth digital terrain models (DTM) while ensuring zero risk to active industrial operations.

Engineering Solutions Implemented

Utilized multi-return aerial LiDAR sensor payloads coupled with high-precision RTK GNSS ground control networks. Applied automated Python post-processing scripts to filter point clouds, classify ground versus non-ground returns, and extract volumetric cut/fill measurements with survey-grade repeatability.
