About Taylor Woodall

Mechanical engineer and entrepreneur specializing in aerospace engineering, commercial drone operations, technical visualization, and business leadership.

Executive & Technical Profile

Engineering Precision from Aerospace Propulsion to Aerial Drone Choreography

Graduated with a Bachelor of Science (B.S.) in Mechanical Engineering from the University of San Diego. Throughout my career, I have worked across the bleeding edge of aerospace propulsion, commercial drone ecosystems, and spatial computing.

My engineering background includes contributing directly to NASA's Space Launch System (SLS) program at Aerojet Rocketdyne, focusing on the iconic RS-25 rocket engines and combustion device systems. In 2019, I founded Skyworx, LLC to pioneer commercial aerial data acquisition, thermal inspections, and aerial LiDAR mapping for critical infrastructure. In 2022, I expanded our operations by launching Skyworx Drone Shows, directing hundreds of synchronized drones in complex 3D airspace while upholding rigorous FAA flight safety standards.

I specialize in translating technical concepts into practical solutions through spatial data interpretation, visual accuracy, workflow automation in Python, and photorealistic 3D modeling in Blender.
